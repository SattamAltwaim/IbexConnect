Knows when
you're off the VPN

      
If sign-in fails the app says why: no VPN, wrong password, unregistered key, changed host key, and what to do about it.

    

  


  

    
      
      Download for Mac
    
    
latest release · macOS 15 or later

    
Unzip, drag to Applications. First launch: right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security. Off campus, connect to the KAUST VPN first.
