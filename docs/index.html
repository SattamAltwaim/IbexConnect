IbexConnect

        
GPU jobs on KAUST's Ibex cluster, from your Mac

      

      
See which GPUs are free, start a Jupyter session or a batch job, watch its output, and get a working localhost link — the SSH tunnel is handled for you.

latest release · macOS 15 or later · free

    

  


  

    
What it does

    

      
sinfo
Free GPUs at a glance
Every GPU type on Ibex with how many are free right now, refreshed every 30 seconds, so you ask for what will actually start.

      
jupyter
Jupyter in one click
Pick GPUs and a time limit. The app submits the job, waits for the node, opens the tunnel, and hands you a link with its token. Paste it into a browser or VS Code.

      
sbatch
Batch jobs
GPUs, time, memory, CPUs, a command. Submitted the way the cluster likes it, with the queue reason explained in plain words while it waits.

      
tail -F
Live output
Your job's stdout and stderr stream into the app as it runs. No more cat slurm-123456.out.

      
rsync
Uploads
Copy a file or a folder from your Mac to your home on Ibex from the toolbar.

      
ssh
Knows why it failed
No VPN, wrong password, unregistered key, changed host key — the login window tells you which, and what to do about it.

    

  


  

    
Install

    

      
1
Download and unzip
Drag IbexConnect.app to your Applications folder.

      
2
Open it the first time
Right-click the app and choose Open. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. This is only needed once.

      
3
Sign in
Your KAUST username and password (kept in your Keychain if you ask), or an SSH key already registered on Ibex.

      
4
Pick a GPU
Choose New Job, pick what you need, and you're on the cluster.

    

    
Off campus? Ibex only accepts connections from inside KAUST. Connect to the KAUST VPN before signing in — the app will remind you if you forget.

  


  

    
    ibex
    Built with the system ssh. No accounts, no telemetry, nothing leaves your Mac except what you send to Ibex.
